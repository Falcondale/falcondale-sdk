## v0.4.0 (2026-10-19)

### Feat

- **clustering**: Horn and Gottlieb quantum clustering and quantum k-means
- SchrodingerClustering, the Quantum Clustering of Horn and Gottlieb, descends samples on the Schrödinger potential of a Parzen wave function, searches a grid of relative widths with the admissibility and silhouette rules of QuantumClustering and assigns unseen samples (Project.cluster("qc"))
- QuantumKMeans, k-means and k-medians (center="median") with sample to center distances estimated by quantum interference of amplitude encoded vectors and shot noise, with transform distances usable as anomaly scores and fuzzy memberships (Project.cluster("qkmeans"))
- interference_circuit returns the Qiskit circuit estimating the distance of a pair of vectors, to run it on hardware (Qiskit is now part of the clustering extra)
- make_clustering_pipeline accepts any clustering estimator
- **qfs**: Improved QUBO formulation

## v0.3.0 (2026-10-19)

### BREAKING CHANGE

- D-Wave Leap cloud access (feature_selection token argument) has been removed, simulated annealing runs locally with dwave-samplers and the QUBO can be retrieved with falcondale.feature_selection.build_qubo
- Python 3.11 to 3.13 are supported, Qiskit 2 and PennyLane 0.45 are required
- the core package only depends on numpy, pandas, scikit-learn and scipy, quantum techniques need their extra: falcondale[feature-selection], falcondale[qml], falcondale[clustering] or falcondale[all]
- falcondale.main is split into falcondale.project and falcondale.model, Dataset.select was removed and unknown methods, models or metrics raise errors
- Project.cluster("pqc") searches a bandwidth grid instead of estimating a single sigma with DensityEstimator, and raises an error when no admissible partition is found

### Feat

- SDK rebuilt on uv, Qiskit 2, PennyLane and local solvers
- QuantumClustering, a scikit-learn clustering estimator that fits APQC on a train split, assigns unseen samples with predict and predict_proba and records every candidate of its bandwidth and energy merge search (#31)
- make_clustering_pipeline chains median imputation, scaling, PCA and QuantumClustering so that preprocessing is fitted on the train split only (#31)
- Project no longer requires a target for unsupervised tasks (#31)
- classical svc baseline available through Project.evaluate("svc")
- all classifiers follow the scikit-learn estimator API, QNN trained with cross entropy returning proper probabilities
- Model.save and Model.load, reproducible solvers through seed and lightweight dataset profiling
- **clustering**: Synthetic clusters added for testing and examples

### Fix

- the max_cols penalty was never added to the QUBO due to an operator precedence bug
- QAOA returned wrong selections due to swapped binary_repr arguments and a Z basis sign mismatch
- simulated bifurcation built its coupling matrix with aliased rows
- missing values were not filled during preprocessing
- recall reported the specificity, both metrics are now available
- PennyLane QSVC models can be saved (#25)
- QSVC kernels are computed from cached statevectors reducing memory and time (#27)
- independent APQC fits in the same process work and are reproducible (#26)
- **tests**: make clustering tests robust to float32 and well count differences
